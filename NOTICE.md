# Provenance and third-party material

This repository documents a local NVIDIA software installation observed on 11 November 2026. It contains analysis results, reconstructed code, extracted interfaces and selected distributed text resources. It is an independent research repository and is not an NVIDIA product or an official NVIDIA source release.

NVIDIA and third-party names identify the software being studied. Their code, resources, interfaces and notices retain their respective ownership and licensing terms. No repository-wide license is asserted over extracted or reconstructed third-party material.

The original executable images, drivers, PDB files and IDA databases are not redistributed here. Source SHA-256 values identify the files analyzed; they do not identify the transformed text artifacts in this repository. The published artifacts have separate digests in `SHA256SUMS.txt`.

Distributed license texts are retained in `evidence/text/` and can be located through `evidence/text_index.json` and `THIRD_PARTY_NOTICES.txt`. No copyright or license comment blocks were encountered in the parsed JavaScript. Configuration files and distributed documentation remain evidence and may contain their original explanatory text.
